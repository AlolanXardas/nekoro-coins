Adds 120 coins for currency, quest icons, shop items or anything else.
Made for my Nekoro Pokemon modpack, but it works with any mods.

Required on the client and the server.

Work in progress

All my projects are being prepared for a big update of my modpack. I'm changing them step by step so they fit together, and they should be ready by the end of this year.
Many graphics are temporary until the mechanics of all my mods are finished.

Found a bug, have an idea, or need this mod on another loader or Minecraft version?
Tell me in the comments or on Discord.

Features

Coins

  • 40 colours, 40 with a symbol and 40 with a cat.
  • The coins do nothing on their own and have no recipes by default.
  • The names are there to tell the coins apart, and some of them might be a little strange.

Item tags

Use these in quests, shops or KubeJS to accept any coin from a group:

  • #nekorocoins:coins: all 120 coins
  • #nekorocoins:color_coins: 001-040
  • #nekorocoins:symbol_coins: 041-080
  • #nekorocoins:cat_coins: 081-120

More information

Config

  • config/Nekoro/Coins/coins.json has one line for each coin, and every option is explained in the file.
  • Change the name of a coin. On a server everyone sees the server's names.
  • Turn a coin off. It leaves the creative tab and loses its recipe, but coins players already have stay.
  • Give a coin a recipe from an item or a tag: how many go in and how many coins come out.
